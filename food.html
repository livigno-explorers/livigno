Food & local

Food and local products

Livigno cooks in the tradition of the Valtellina valley: buckwheat, mountain cheese and cured meat. Try the dishes, meet local producers and shop duty-free.

What to eat and buy

Pizzoccheri

Buckwheat pasta · Valtellina

Short buckwheat pasta cooked with potatoes and cabbage, served with melted cheese and butter.

Sciatt

Cheese fritters

Crispy fritters made of buckwheat batter with a soft cheese centre.

Bresaola

Cured beef

Air-dried, thinly sliced beef from the Valtellina, often served with lemon and oil.

  • Mountain huts (rifugi)

    Simple local food on the slopes and along hiking trails.

  • Traditional restaurants

    Valtellina dishes such as pizzoccheri and sciatt.

  • Pizzerias and cafés

    Quick meals in the village centre.

A list of restaurants with opening hours will be loaded from the tourist office data (planned for the next semester).

Why are prices lower in Livigno?

Livigno is a duty-free area: Italian VAT is not charged on most goods. This is why many visitors shop for sports equipment, perfume or chocolate here. Check the customs allowances before you travel home.

Local producers

  • Dairy

    Fresh milk, mountain cheese and yoghurt from the valley.

  • Honey and mountain herbs

    Products of the high alpine meadows.

  • Bakery

    Bread and sweets made with buckwheat and rye.
