Travel information

  • By car

    From Italy over the Foscagno Pass; from Switzerland through the Munt la Schera tunnel or, in summer, over the Forcola di Livigno pass.

  • By bus

    Bus connections from Tirano and Bormio, which can be reached by train or car.

  • By plane

    The nearest large airports are in Milan, Bergamo and Zurich; continue by train, bus or car.

    Frequently asked questions

    When is the best time to visit?

    Winter for skiing and snowboarding, summer for hiking and cycling. Spring and autumn are quieter.

    Do I need a car in Livigno?

    Not necessarily: the village can be explored on foot and by local bus.

    Is everything duty-free?

    Most goods are sold without Italian VAT, but customs limits apply when you leave. Check them before you travel.
